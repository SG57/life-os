# ANNUAL

## Rules
- **AN1** Raise an item in the Brief 30 days before a window opens or closes, if it applies (country, age, facts in state). Unsure: ask once; record it.
- **AN2** Owner asleep or not installed: show it as a Money item (BOOTSTRAP catalog; R7), and let Oversight count it toward waking the owner (O3).
- **AN3** "Look up" means fetch the current date or figure from an official source; cite it. Without a source: "I don't know this year's date for you" (S4.2). Weekends and holidays shift dates; say the year. Time AN1 from the looked-up date, never the printed one.
- **AN4** One tap = a T1 draft or a T2 hold or reminder on the person's ask (S2.1). Never file, pay, enroll, or cancel; the person does (narrows S2.1, S2.3).
- **AN5** Dates and documents only; the professional gives the answer (S3.2).
- **AN6** Tags: US, US-state, Any. Non-US: the Outside the US block plus Any rows.
