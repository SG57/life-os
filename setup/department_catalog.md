# Department catalog — the data the scaffolding runs on

Canonical for Life OS 3.1.0. The setup's scaffolding step (setup/DEPARTMENT_SCAFFOLDING.md) maps a person's life map against this catalog and sorts every department into one of three lists: **build now**, **sleep at HQ** (with its wake trigger recorded), or **not applicable**. Nothing here is invented per-person — remits and wake triggers are fixed; intake questions are the department's starting set, tailored by the setup interview.

Two catalog entries are not departments and never become chats: **Good Life** and **Oversight** — they live in HQ. Good Life: one thing to look forward to, every week. Oversight: HQ watches every department, wakes and retires them, and reports framework bugs to the maker with the person's yes.

## Always on (build for everyone)

### Health
- Watches: due care at every age (doctor, dentist, eyes, ears), refills, results, records.
- Intake: Who are your current doctor, dentist, eye doctor — or do you need them found? Insurance through whom? Any conditions, medications, allergies? When were your last physical, cleaning, eye exam? Anything you're avoiding booking?

### Home
- Watches: the place you live — lease or mortgage paperwork, property tax, association notices, deposits, valuations.
- Intake: Own or rent — and what's the payment and due date? Who insures the home, renewing when? Property tax schedule? Any HOA? Who handles what at the property?

### Identity & scams
- Watches: credit in your name, account takeovers, tax-refund scams, phone/email hijack attempts.
- Intake: Are your credit files frozen at all three bureaus? Password manager — which? Two-factor on email, banking, phone carrier? Any fraud scare or incident before?

### Insurance & benefits
- Watches: every policy and election window — health, home, auto, life, disability — no lapses, no missed windows, beneficiaries as intended.
- Intake: List every policy you carry (carrier, kind, renewal). Who are the beneficiaries? When are your open-enrollment windows? Any coverage you know is missing?

### Money
- Watches: bills on time, balances, the cushion, where spare money goes.
- Intake: Bank accounts and cards — where, and which is primary? Monthly bills list with due dates? Income — sources and pay rhythm? How much cushion do you want to hold? Anything you're charged for that you don't use?

### Papers & IDs
- Watches: IDs, passport, vital records, civic dates (voting, jury duty, registrations), name/address changes in the right order.
- Intake: Passport — have one, expiry? Driver's license expiry? Where are your birth certificate and Social Security card kept? Registered to vote at your current address? Anything expired right now?

### Taxes
- Watches: every return and estimated-payment date, withholding matched to your life, forms in hand before filing day.
- Intake: How do you file — software, accountant, by hand? Who prepared last year's return? Can you share your last two or three returns? Any IRS or state notices? Income sources beyond a paycheck? Anything unusual this year (marriage, sale, move, new business)?

### Upkeep & safety
- Watches: alarms, home systems, hidden hazards (radon, mold, lead, well water), warranties and recalls on things you own.
- Intake: HVAC, water heater, roof — ages and last service? Smoke/CO alarms tested when? Appliance warranties active? Security system or cameras? Anything broken you're living with?

### Will & wishes
- Watches: will/trust, beneficiaries, advance directives, the one packet that says where everything is.
- Intake: Any will or trust today? Who would you name as executor? Who takes the pets? Medical wishes and who speaks for you? Where would your family start — is there an asset list anywhere?

## Build when the life map shows them

### Business & side income
- Watches: money earned outside a paycheck — kept legal, separate, insured, priced right, taxed on time.
- Wakes on: starting freelance or side income, forming an LLC or company, renting out a property or room, hiring help.
- Intake: What are the income streams? Sole proprietor or LLC? Business money kept separate? Any 1099s expected? Who does the books?

### Debts & credit
- Watches: every loan's plan, promo-rate flips, co-signing decisions, credit picture.
- Wakes on: leaving school with student loans, first credit card, asked to co-sign, planning a mortgage or car loan.
- Intake: Every debt — creditor, balance, rate, payment, due date? Any promo rates with end dates? Co-signed anything? Planned borrowing in the next two years?

### Digital life
- Watches: photos, files, accounts, domains — surviving a lost phone, dead drive, or hospital stay; a chosen person can reach what's needed.
- Wakes on: new phone or computer.
- Intake: Where do photos and files live, and are they backed up twice? Domains owned — registrar, renewal dates? Password manager and who has emergency access? Accounts you'd most regret losing?

### Emergencies
- Watches: the leave-tonight plan — go-bags, papers, animals, insurance matched to local risks, claim steps in order.
- Wakes on: severe weather warning or evacuation order, home damage, break-in.
- Intake: Go-bags packed — where? Evacuation destination? Pet carriers ready? What gets grabbed in the first five minutes? Local risks (fire, flood, quake, storm)?

### Occasions & giving
- Watches: birthdays, holidays, weddings being planned, gifts and charitable giving — planned, budgeted, receipted.
- Wakes on: engagement.
- Intake: Key dates list — whose birthdays and anniversaries count? Gift style and budget norms? Charitable causes and annual gifts? Celebrations already on the horizon?

### Pets
- Watches: animals cared for on time, costs planned, a named person ready to step in tomorrow.
- Wakes on: new pet.
- Intake: Who are the animals — species, breed, age, weight? Vet — who and where? Vaccines and prevention schedule? Food, meds, supplements? Insurance? Who steps in if you can't?

### Retirement
- Watches: every match dollar captured, no old account lost, earnings record right, one-way choices made with a professional.
- Wakes on: first job, new job, a retirement or investment account opened or found, stock grant or options, turning 50.
- Intake: Every retirement account — employer plans now, old ones from past jobs, IRAs? Current contribution rates and match formulas? Any Roth anywhere? Age you'd like the option to stop?

### Vehicles
- Watches: every vehicle legal, insured, maintained; recalls fixed; registrations on time.
- Wakes on: buying or leasing a car, a child turning 16.
- Intake: Vehicles — year, make, model, mileage? Registration due dates? Insurance carrier? Service history and where work gets done? Open recalls?

### Work
- Watches: pay, benefits, time off, equity, licenses, leave, rights — from first offer to last day.
- Wakes on: first job, job offer, offer abroad, new job, job loss.
- Intake: Employer(s), role, pay, pay rhythm? Benefits elections and deadlines? Equity, bonuses, review cycle? Contracts signed — non-compete, IP? Anything with a deadline (vesting, leave, licenses)?

## Sleep until life wakes them (HQ holds the entry + trigger)

### After a loss
- Watches: post-death paperwork in the right order, at a bearable pace; deadlines met; grief never touched.
- Wakes on: death of someone close or a spouse/partner, being named executor.

### Benefits
- Watches: public benefits applied for, renewed, and reported on time.
- Wakes on: job loss, big income drop, disability or long illness, a benefits letter.

### Buying or selling a home
- Watches: pre-approval to keys — contingencies, rate lock, title, insurance, transfer details.
- Wakes on: starting to shop for a home, selling, inheriting one.

### Caregiving
- Watches: a loved one's papers, appointments, benefits, money questions, safety logistics — organized, so the caregiver isn't carrying it all in their head. Logistics and records only; their clinicians decide medical questions.
- Wakes on: a parent or relative needs care, someone leaves the hospital, being named someone's agent.
- Intake (on waking): Who needs care, and where do they live? Who's their local support? Care team and coverage? What do they handle themselves, and what's slipping?

### Extra support
- Watches: programs, school plans, waitlists, and benefits that come with a disability or long illness — applied early, renewed on time.
- Wakes on: disability or long illness, a child's developmental or disability diagnosis, a dependent with a disability turning 17.

### Kids
- Watches: coverage windows, documents, checkups, childcare and school dates, money accounts, guardians.
- Wakes on: a child joins the family, a stepchild joins the household, raising a relative's child.

### Later life
- Watches: living where you want, independently, with future care decided in advance and older-age paperwork handled.
- Wakes on: turning 70, retiring within 12 months, a fall, starting to live alone, death of a spouse, giving up driving.

### Legal papers
- Watches: any paper with a deadline from a court, agency, or other side — read, dated, met, with a professional deciding.
- Wakes on: legal paper arrives, severance agreement offered, eviction or foreclosure notice, an arrest, a dispute worth taking further.
- Intake (on waking): The paper itself — photograph or scan it. What date is printed on it? Anything signed already?

### Medical bills
- Watches: no bill paid before it's matched to what the insurer says is owed; appeal and assistance windows never close unseen.
- Wakes on: a procedure or hospital stay, a claim denied, a bill over the person's threshold.

### Military & veterans
- Watches: protections and benefits earned through service, claimed on time.
- Wakes on: joining or deploying, leaving service, a veteran in the household, a veteran parent needing care, death of a veteran in the family.

### Moving
- Watches: the dated checklist from eight weeks out to sixty days after — notice, movers, mail, money, coverage, records, animals, medicines follow you.
- Wakes on: moving, moving state or abroad, lease not renewed, home under contract.

### School
- Watches: aid, tuition, refund, and savings deadlines for you or a child.
- Wakes on: enrolling in school or training, a child within two years of college or trade school, a child leaving for college, a professional exam.

### Separation
- Watches: accounts, cover, beneficiaries, documents, passwords, and court dates untangled safely, in the right order — nothing moved before an attorney weighs in.
- Wakes on: separation, family-court papers served.

### Status papers
- Watches: the right to live, work, and travel where you are — visa and permit dates, address reports, forms.
- Wakes on: holding a visa or residence permit, moving abroad, a job offer abroad.

### Trips
- Watches: documents valid, entry permission in hand, cover abroad, medicines packed, refund dates tracked, home and animals handled.
- Wakes on: a trip abroad booked, moving abroad, a job offer abroad.
- Intake (on waking): Where, when, how long? Passport validity? Who covers home and animals? Bookings and their refund deadlines?

### Us
- Watches: when two lives combine — shared bills, accounts, leases, titles, names, insurance, papers naming each other — settled in the right order, sharing only what both said yes to.
- Wakes on: moving in together, engagement, marriage, both partners asking.
- Intake (on waking): What's already shared, what's deliberately separate? Names changing? Papers that should name each other (beneficiaries, emergency contacts)? What stays private by agreement?
